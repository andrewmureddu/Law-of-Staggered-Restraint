## 1. The Big Picture: What We Have Actually Proved Together

Looking back across the arc from Cantor continuation trees ([Leap-and-Stride Search & First Signal Law Continuation Trees](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YEJtM6zoM1Dhd7u-nki7Un_7_38AIMvVkaYq5XkwOsg/edit?usp=drivesdk&ouid=112427431233984459040)) and quantum records ([Diffusive_records_of_a_thermal_qubit_v0_8.pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Zm6qPofBUZD4ACysL84CWb1IxYW3jC20/view?usp=drivesdk)) to three-dimensional Navier–Stokes turbulence ([Geometric_Restraint_Navier_Stokes_Research_Plan.docx](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BEC0YFTlFE-EEAZhEfnAsXHBXcSp7dKI/view?usp=drivesdk)), the single most important breakthrough is not any single regression score—it is the **discovery of a self-correcting scientific engine**.

Every time our multi-agent loop proposed a seductive mathematical construction, we paired it with a pre-registered falsifier that stripped away what was redundant and exposed a sharper physical law underneath:

- **From "Two Special Observables" to Exact Clock Derivatives:** When $(T_4^+, V_3^+)$ improved stretching forecasts by $+31.9\%$, the Conditional Schur Complement test in Checkpoint III proved that the turnover sum $R = T_4^+ + V_3^+$ explained only $0.00066\%$ of additional variance once the exact derivative $J = T_4^+ - V_3^+ = \dot{P}_+$ and strain-capacity rate $\dot{Q} = T_4^Q - V_3^Q$ were included.
- **From "Efficiency Decline = Vortex Restraint" to the Three Clocks and the Four-Term Race:** Differentiating $\|S\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ without eigenvectors via the Sylvester–Hellmann–Feynman theorem proved that a vortex loses stretching efficiency (Clock 1: $H < 0$) while positive stretching (Clock 2: $\dot{P}_+ > 0$) and enstrophy (Clock 3: $\dot{Z} > 0$) are still growing—and that the Clock-1 downturn is driven by inviscid triad strain-capacity outrunning ($\Delta_{\mathrm{triad}} < 0$) while differential viscosity ($-\Delta_{\mathrm{visc}} > 0$) actually opposes the efficiency drop.
- **From "Prefix Stranding" in Fourier Shells to Scale & Dimensional Covariance:** When fixed integer wavenumber shells collapsed under Leave-One-Family-Out (LOFO) transfer ($+40.04\% \to +7.01\%$) and raw second derivatives $\dot{H}$ collapsed on time-to-turn $\tau_{\mathrm{turn}}$ ($-41.05\%$), normalizing wavevectors by the Taylor wavenumber $k_{\mathrm{char}}(u) = \sqrt{D/(\nu W)}$ inside smooth Partition-of-Unity filters and normalizing $\dot{H}$ into the timescale-covariant ratio $\tau_{\mathrm{kin}} = -H / \dot{H}$ restored LOFO transfer to $+32.99\%$ on $\eta_+$ and $+43.50\%$ on $\tau_{\mathrm{turn}}$ ($+84.55$ percentage points).
- **From Smooth Bulk Calculus to Dual Singular Switching Surfaces:** Auditing the exact second-order crossing slope $\dot{H}(t_*) = \ddot{P}_+/P_+ - \ddot{Q}/Q$ at $H(t_*) = 0$ revealed two positive distributional corner fluxes across codimension-1 surfaces in the fluid: the zero-stretching surface $\partial\Omega_+ = \{p = 0\}$ ($+0.10798$) and the planar-shear switching surface $\Sigma_0 = \{\lambda_2(S) = 0\} = \{\det S = 0\}$ ($+0.09727$).

## 2. Four Concrete Experiments for the Next Phase (Experiments 015–018)

| Experiment ID & Title | Core Mathematical Question | Pre-Registered Falsifier | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Experiment 015: The Lag Between the Clocks** | Can Clock 1 (efficiency peak $t_1^*$) predict when Clock 2 (stretching peak $t_2^*$) and Clock 3 (enstrophy peak $t_3^*$) will strike? | If the kinematic lag ratio $\Delta t_{1\to 2}^{\mathrm{kin}} = -J(t_1^*) / \dot{J}(t_1^*)$ fails to beat a family-timescale baseline on $64^3$ grids across $\nu \in [0.002, 0.05]$. | Turns Clock 1 (which fires early while the vortex is still growing) into a quantitative lead-time warning sensor for peak vortex stretching. |
| **Experiment 016: The Schur–Cantor Wavelet Tree** | How many Fourier shells should an observer resolve at a given Reynolds number without triggering prefix stranding? | If recursive Partition-of-Unity shell splitting gated by conditional Schur partial $R^2$ fails to outperform fixed 3-shell PoU at $N = 64^3$ and $128^3$. | Directly unifies the Cantor First Signal Law with 3D Navier–Stokes turbulence. |
| **Experiment 017: Topological Surgery of the Planar-Shear Surface $\Sigma_0$** | What is the physical meaning of the distributional corner flux $\mathcal{B}_{\Sigma_0}^Q = \langle|\omega|^2 \delta(\lambda_2)(\dot{\lambda}_2)^2\rangle$ across $\det S(x) = 0$? | If $\mathcal{B}_{\Sigma_0}^Q / Q$ shows no correlation with vortex-tube to vortex-sheet topological conversion during Kida–Pelz and antiparallel reconnection. | Links the analytical non-smoothness of $\|S\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ directly to the classical problem of tube-to-sheet transition in 3D turbulence. |
| **Experiment 018: Minimum-Energy Catalytic Steering of Restraint** | Can we steer a growing vortex into early self-restraint using a tiny ($<0.1\%$ energy) perturbation in the nullspace of $(P_+, Q, J, \dot{Q})$? | If the constrained Fréchet minimizer $v^* = \arg\min_{\ker D(P_+,Q,J,\dot{Q})} D(\dot{H})[v]$ fails to advance $t_1^*$ and reduce peak $P_+$ in 3D DNS. | Moves our program from passive forecasting of vortex restraint to active, closed-loop geometric control of turbulent amplification. |
